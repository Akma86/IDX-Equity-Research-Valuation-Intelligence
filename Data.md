# ICBP Equity Research & Valuation Intelligence

## 1. Project Overview

This project aims to conduct a comprehensive equity research and valuation analysis of **PT Indofood CBP Sukses Makmur Tbk (ICBP)**.

The objective is to understand the company's financial performance, business fundamentals, industry dynamics, macroeconomic exposure, and market valuation to determine whether its stock is potentially undervalued, fairly valued, or overvalued.

Rather than relying solely on stock price movements or conventional financial ratios, this project combines financial statement analysis, quantitative analysis, macroeconomic indicators, peer comparison, and intrinsic valuation.

### Key Research Questions

1. Is ICBP's business performance improving or deteriorating?
2. How sustainable is the company's revenue and earnings growth?
3. How efficient is ICBP in managing its assets, debt, and capital?
4. What are the primary factors driving ICBP's revenue, profitability, and margins?
5. How sensitive is ICBP to macroeconomic conditions and commodity price fluctuations?
6. How does ICBP compare with its industry peers?
7. Is ICBP currently undervalued, fairly valued, or overvalued?
8. What are the potential risks and catalysts that could affect its future performance?

## 3. DCF Valuation Requirements

In addition to historical financial data, the project requires forward-looking assumptions to estimate ICBP's intrinsic value using a Discounted Cash Flow (DCF) model.

### 3.1 Required Forecast Variables

| Variable | Description |
|---|---|
| Revenue Growth | Expected annual revenue growth |
| EBIT Margin | Expected operating profitability |
| Tax Rate | Effective corporate tax rate |
| D&A | Depreciation and amortization |
| Capital Expenditure | Expected investment in fixed assets |
| Working Capital Changes | Changes in operating working capital |
| WACC | Weighted Average Cost of Capital |
| Risk-Free Rate | Government bond yield benchmark |
| Equity Risk Premium | Additional return required for equity risk |
| Beta | Systematic market risk |
| Cost of Debt | Company's borrowing cost |
| Terminal Growth Rate | Long-term cash flow growth assumption |
| Net Debt | Total debt minus cash and cash equivalents |
| Shares Outstanding | Number of outstanding shares |

### 3.2 Valuation Scenarios

Three scenarios will be developed to account for uncertainty.

| Assumption | Bear Case | Base Case | Bull Case |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Revenue Growth | Conservative | Expected | Optimistic |
| EBIT Margin | Lower | Normalized | Higher |
| Terminal Growth | Lower | Moderate | Higher |
| WACC | Higher | Expected | Lower |

All assumptions must be supported by historical performance, company guidance, industry conditions, and reasonable economic expectations.

**Final valuation outputs:**

- Enterprise Value
- Equity Value
- Intrinsic Value per Share
- Current Market Price
- Upside / Downside Potential
- Margin of Safety
- Sensitivity Analysis

| [Trading Economics](https://tradingeconomics.com/) | Economic indicators and commodity prices |
| [FRED](https://fred.stlouisfed.org/) | International economic and financial indicators |

**Data collection principles:**

1. Prioritize official company disclosures and regulatory filings.
2. Record the source and retrieval date for every dataset.
3. Maintain consistent reporting periods and currency units.
4. Distinguish between quarterly, nine-month, and full-year financial figures.
5. Avoid mixing adjusted and unadjusted stock prices.
6. Document missing values and estimation assumptions.
7. Preserve original raw data before performing transformations.

## 8. Research Philosophy

The objective is not to predict stock prices using indicators alone.

The research should establish a logical connection between:

**Business Fundamentals → Financial Performance → Earnings Expectations → Market Valuation → Investment Decision**

Every investment conclusion must be supported by evidence, transparent assumptions, and an assessment of potential downside risks.

The final goal is to think and work like an equity research analyst: understand the business, identify what drives its financial performance, evaluate its intrinsic value, challenge the market's expectations, and formulate an evidence-based investment thesis.
